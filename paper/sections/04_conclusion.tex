\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

PMC corrects cross-modal centroid mismatch in compressed ANN with a build-time, database-side shift that rewrites routing pivots and sign-bit boundaries at the source, yielding gains that scale with modality-gap magnitude at no extra index memory and, at $\alpha{=}1$, no serving-time query transform. The one regime favoring uncorrected codes---deep reranking under sub-1\% list probing at 400M scale---is a scan-depth effect. Future work: drift-adaptive $\alpha$, graph-based ANN, and billion-scale deployment.
